Christian Pure Bible Search

A free app that puts the whole Bible and our prayers on your own computer. Type what you are carrying, such as worry, grief or a hard decision, and it shows you what Scripture says about it and a prayer you can pray. Nothing you type leaves your computer, and it works with no internet connection.

This page shows the app; it runs on your own computer rather than here. Installing it takes a few minutes and needs no account.

Get it free on GitHub

A search for anxiety.

What it does

  • Finds the passages for 6,699 subjects, from anxiety and forgiveness to money worries and marriage, and suggests close subjects to read next.
  • Opens any verse or chapter you type, such as John 3:16 or Psalm 23, and lets you read on chapter by chapter.
  • Offers a prayer from Christian Pure for the need, with a link to the article it comes from.
  • Keeps your searches private. They are never sent anywhere, so you can bring the hardest questions to it.

Install it

Choose the way that suits your computer.

  • Pinokio (Windows, Mac and Linux): open Pinokio, choose Download from URL, paste https://github.com/christianpure/bible-search, then click Install and Start.
  • A home server: we have sent it to the Umbrel, CasaOS and Unraid app stores. Until it appears there, the Docker line below works on any of them.
  • Docker: run this line, then open localhost:8417 in your browser. docker run -d --name christianpure-bible-search -p 8417:8417 -v christianpure-bible-search:/data ghcr.io/christianpure/bible-search
  • The code: everything is on GitHub, free to read and run.

Where the words come from

The Bible text is the Berean Standard Bible, which is in the public domain. The subjects and their verses come from OpenBible.info (CC BY 4.0), with the verses chosen on our own verse pages shown first. The prayers are the ones you can read on christianpure.com, and the app checks for new ones once a week when it is online.
